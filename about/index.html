Zee Pauli

About

Zee Pauli is a designer working across branding, packaging, and digital and physical product experiences — with a focus on work that feels intentional and considered.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, her practice combines editorial restraint with material craft, bringing brand and packaging work to life through both physical production and digital execution.
